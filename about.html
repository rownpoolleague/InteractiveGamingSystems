Built by an Operator, Designed for Your Players

Interactive Gaming Systems didn't start in a tech-incubator or a corporate boardroom. It started on league night. As a pool league manager handling active rosters, handicap tracking, and schedules, I experienced firsthand the frustration of using outdated paper sheets and rigid, expensive software platforms that didn't fit our local rules.

I realized that local tournament networks and tavern leagues were being completely ignored by major software developers. Big tech companies build cookie-cutter programs and force *your* organization to change how it operates just to fit their system. I knew there was a better way—so I built it.

Our Development Philosophy: Lean Logic

We prioritize speed, cross-device performance, and clean utility over heavy visual fluff. On a busy match night, players want instant phone access to leaderboards, active pairings, and standings without slow screen loads or confusing navigation menus. It should just work.

My career has been entirely built on listening to clients, communicating transparently, and ensuring people get exactly what they pay for.
