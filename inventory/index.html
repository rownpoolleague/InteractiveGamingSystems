How to Use

1. Search: Start typing in the search bar to find an item. Click the item to begin the item count.

2. Item Entry: Enter the values for each unit type i.e. "Case", "Bag", "Each", etc. Click save. You may adjust your number at any time, even after saving.

3. Flag item to order: Checking the box will create a record you can print as a reminder to order items.

4. Review Inventory: This screen shows a full list of all saved items in FMS order. Click an item here to adjust the count if needed. You will also see a list of your flagged items at the bottom. Please print as a reminder to order. Inventory list clears at 4a.m. but you will have to click "Clear Order Flags" to remove the order list.

SEARCH TIP: Items are named strategically. For instance, when counting in the freezer type "Dough", after you save an item, the dough list is reloaded; the word "Cup" will produce all sauce cups etc.
