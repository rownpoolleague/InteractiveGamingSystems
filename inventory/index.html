How to Use

1. Search: Start typing in the search bar to find an item. Click the item.

2. Count: Enter values for each unit type i.e. "Case", "Bag", "Each", etc. Click save. You may adjust your number at any time, even after saving.

3. Calculator: Tap the icon to add to your already saved total. Useful at EOD when adding in uncounted items. (Loose Boxes, maketable items and so on)

4. Review Inventory: This screen shows a full list of all saved items in FMS order. Click an item here to adjust the count if needed.

5. Save to Phone: Downloads a text list to your phone.

6. Clear All: DELETES all saved data. Do this ONLY AFTER completing inventory in FMS.

SEARCH TIP: Items are named strategically. For instance, when counting in the freezer type "Dough", after you save an item, the dough list is reloaded; the word "Cup" will produce all sauce cups etc.
