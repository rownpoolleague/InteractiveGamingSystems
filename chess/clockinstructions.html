Clock Instructions

Setting the Time

  • Tap fields to set Minutes, Increment and Delay as needed. Tap SET
  • Tap START to enter full screen mode

    • Starting the Clock

  • Once in full screen mode a players time may be started by tapping the opposite side clock face.
  • Any increment set, will be added to your time after you finish your move.
  • Any delay set will immediately be seen counting down above the main time interface.
  • When your turn is over, begin the other players clock by tapping anywhere on your side of the screen

    • Pausing/Accessing Menu

  • Long press (1.5s) anywhere EXCEPT the clock numbers to pause and access the clock menu, time will remain paused while the menu is open. The director should add 2 seconds extra to the clock that was running when it was paused.

    • Menu Controls

  • WARNING!! Clicking SET while in pause mode will "Set" to the ORIGINAL values in the minutes, inc and delay fields.
  • Individual times may be adjusted by directly tapping and adjusting the minutes or seconds
  • Tap START to resume the full screen clock, times will remain paused until the screen is tapped.
