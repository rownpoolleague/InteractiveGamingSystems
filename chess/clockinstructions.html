Clock Instructions

Setting the Time

  • When the menu is visible, tap the Minutes or Seconds fields directly to edit the time.
  • Use the top bar to set your desired Minutes (base time), Increment (added time per move), and Delay.

Starting a Match

  • Once configured, tap the START button. This will hide the settings and enter fullscreen mode.
  • The clock remains paused until the first player taps their side of the screen.

During Play

  • Tap your side of the screen to end your turn and start your opponent's clock.
  • If an increment is set, it will be added to your time after you finish your move.

Accessing the Menu

  • To pause the clock or adjust settings during a game, press and hold the right edge of the screen (the "Unlock Zone") for 1.5 seconds.
  • This will reopen the settings menu. Tap START to resume the game, or SET to reset the clock to the initial values.
