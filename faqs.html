Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, fluff-free answers about how our custom software systems work.

Can the software be customized with our league's unique branding and logos?

Yes, completely. We don't hide your identity behind our corporate branding. Your custom web app will feature your league's official logos, preferred color schemes, and tournament graphics. To your players and venue sponsors, it will look and feel like a proprietary digital platform built exclusively for your organization.

Do our players need to download an app from the App Store or Google Play?

No. We build responsive web applications. Players simply visit a regular web link on their phones, bookmark it, and it functions exactly like a native mobile app. This eliminates password resets, download barriers, and device compatibility headaches on busy match nights.

Our league uses highly unique handicap and scoring formulas. Can your system handle that?

Yes, absolutely. Unlike rigid corporate platforms that force you to adapt to their cookie-cutter setups, we specialize in hard-coding your specific mathematical logic. Whether your scoring relies on performance-based adjustments, lag win qualifiers, or complex historical stat weighting, the software adapts entirely to your rulebook.

How does the data connect to our existing records? Can we still use Excel?

We build our pipelines to seamlessly sync directly back into consumer spreadsheet tools like Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel. Your players submit data via a clean mobile interface on game night, and the data automatically updates your master back-end records. You retain full ownership and control of your files without a learning curve.

What happens if a player makes a data entry error or scores a match incorrectly?

You retain full control of your existing records and can still adjust as needed. If building from the ground up you will have full edit access to your newly created database.

Do you charge per player or per team? How does pricing work?

We do not believe in sneaky corporate subscription models that penalize you for growing your league. We charge a transparent, competitive flat fee tailored to the initial build out and specific integrations your venue needs. Once your system is up, you don't get nickel-and-dimed for adding teams.

How do we get started if we want a custom system built?

The process is simple: drop us a line through our contact page. We will schedule a quick conversation to look over your current rulebooks, sheets, or layout ideas. From there, we build a working prototype so you can see exactly how it functions on a phone before making any commitments.
